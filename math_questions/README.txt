Math Question Bank (Grades 1-5)
================================
Structure: grade_N/<topic>.csv
Columns:   question, answer, difficulty_level (1-3), difficulty_label (easy/medium/hard)
~30 questions per file (10 per difficulty level), ~770 questions total.

Topics by grade:
  Grade 1: addition, subtraction, counting_and_comparing, basic_geometry
  Grade 2: addition, subtraction, intro_multiplication, time_and_money, basic_geometry
  Grade 3: addition_and_subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions, basic_geometry
  Grade 4: multiplication, division, fractions, decimals, basic_algebra, basic_geometry
  Grade 5: multiplication_and_division, fractions, decimals, basic_algebra, basic_geometry, speed_distance_time

Notes:
- Fraction answers are simplified (e.g. "2/3"); division-with-remainder answers look like "13 remainder 2".
- Difficulty levels step up within each topic to match the age range within a grade.
